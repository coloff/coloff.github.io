About Coloff Curtains LLC

Craftsmanship That Complements Your Space

Coloff Curtains LLC is a Musaffah, Abu Dhabi-based provider of custom interior solutions. We create made-to-measure curtains, carpets, sofas and blinds for homes, offices and commercial spaces.

Our work is built around careful measurement, premium materials, skilled craftsmanship and professional finishing. We work closely with clients to understand their style, functional needs and project requirements.
